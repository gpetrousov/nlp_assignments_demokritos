\subsection*{2}

Token-level metrics evaluate Named Entity Recognition (NER) by assessing each individual token, while entity-level metrtrics evaluate spans of tokens in a sentence. Token-level evaluation is simpler and lenient to mistakes, while entity-level evaluation is more strict and requires precise boundaries between entities.

In our situation, entity-level evaluation is preferred, since our task is to classify entities inside sentences. So, exact boundaries and strict identification might require tokens to span across many words (example "New York" instead of "New" and "York").

\subsection*{4}

The function moves through the sentence word-by-word and assigns each token a numeric ID. This ID maps to a specific label stored in the \lstinline{label_id} variable. The table below shows the relationship between each token ID, its corresponding label ID, and its specific function\footnote{\url{https://huggingface.co/learn/llm-course/chapter7/2}}:

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2} % Adds a bit of breathing room between rows
\begin{tabular}{|l|c|p{8cm}|} % Adjust 8cm to fit your page width
\hline
\multicolumn{1}{|c|}{\textbf{Label}} & \textbf{ID} & \textbf{Function} \\ \hline
\textbf{O} & 8 & Indicates the word does not correspond to any entity. \\ \hline
\textbf{B-PER/I-PER} & 3/7 & Indicates the word corresponds to the beginning of or is inside a person entity. \\ \hline
\textbf{B-ORG/I-ORG} & 2/6 & Indicates the word corresponds to the beginning of or is inside an organization entity. \\ \hline
\textbf{B-LOC/I-LOC} & 0/4 & Indicates the word corresponds to the beginning of or is inside a location entity. \\ \hline
\textbf{B-MISC/I-MISC} & 1/5 & Indicates the word corresponds to the beginning of or is inside a miscellaneous entity. \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The value $-100$ is used to ignore specific tokens, such as CLS, SEP, padding tokens, etc., in the loss calculation in order to prevent weight calculations.
